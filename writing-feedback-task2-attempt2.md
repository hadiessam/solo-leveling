```

this concert and how they have been invited to it too, and after a short talk,
Concert host took the microphone and started to talk about how he enjoy his time
in this concert.
```

Split it like this:

```
So they moved around and started talking to their friends, Mohamed and Suzan.
They talked about the concert and how they had been invited too.
After a short talk, the concert host took the microphone. He started to talk
about how much he had enjoyed his time at the concert.
```

Same content. 4 sentences instead of 1.

### 2. Verbs still slipping into the present

| You wrote | Should be |
|---|---|
| how they **have been** invited | how they **had been** invited |
| how he **enjoy** his time | how he **had enjoyed** his time |
| by **said** | by **saying** |

### 3. Missing subject — sentence 4

```
Continued his talk by said its like a dream...
```

There is no person doing the action. English needs one:

> **He** continued his talk by saying it was like a dream...

### 4. Sentence 3 is not a sentence

```
Also how he saw all his friends in one place.
```

This has no main verb and no subject — it is a fragment. Join it to the sentence before:

> ...and **he also said** how he had seen all his friends in one place.
