## THE ONE BIG THING: your whole text is one sentence

Look at this:

```
...crocodiles, we really have fun their and also we visit the gift shop there and
purchase a gifts for eachother and play some games that if we win in it we will
take a big teddy doll, and after that we visited the cafe...
```

**172 words. 0 full stops. 3 commas.**

That is one single sentence running for 172 words. It never stops.

This is not a small detail. In the CEFR, this is the single biggest thing separating
A2 from B1 in writing. A B1 writer controls sentence boundaries. An A2 writer does not
yet. That is literally the difference the examiner is measuring.

**Why it happened:** in Arabic, you can join clauses with "و" and keep going almost
indefinitely. It is grammatically fine there. In English it is not. English needs
full stops, and then a capital letter, and then a new sentence.

**The fix is mechanical, not intellectual.** You do not need to learn new grammar.
You need to put a full stop where your voice already pauses. Read your own text out
loud and stop where you take a breath. That is where the full stop goes.

## ERROR LIST — grouped so you can fix a whole category at once

### 1. TENSE — you kept switching between past and present

You are telling a story about **today**, which is finished. So everything should be
past tense. But you wrote:

| You wrote | Should be |
|---|---|
| I **have** really a very good time | I **had** a really good time |
| we **visit** the gift shop | we **visited** the gift shop |
| we **purchase** a gifts | we **bought** gifts |
| we **play** some games | we **played** some games |
| we **will take** a big teddy doll | the winner **took** home a teddy bear |
| we **drink** some coffee | we **drank** / **had** some coffee |
| we **walks** a lot | we **had walked** a lot |
| we **dance** a lot | we **danced** a lot |
| that **make** us have a lot of fun | that **made** us have fun |

**This is the second-biggest issue.** Note that "we walks" is wrong twice — wrong tense
AND wrong verb form (we walk, not we walks).

**Rule to remember:** if the story happened and finished, every verb is past.
No exceptions, no switching.

### 2. SPELLING

| Wrong | Right |
|---|---|
| actully | **actually** |
| coffe | **coffee** |
| eachother | **each other** (two words) |
| experiance | **experience** |
| fan | **fun** |
| their | **there** |
| i (lowercase) | **I** — always capital, 3 times you wrote it lowercase |

Note: `their` vs `there` — **their** = belonging to them, **there** = a place.
You meant the place. So: *"we really had fun there"*.

### 3. ARTICLE + PLURAL — "a" with a plural noun

| You wrote | Should be |
|---|---|
| a new animal**s** | new animals / several animals |
| a gift**s** | gifts |
| a special event**s** | a special event |
| a very good vibe**s** | a great atmosphere |

**Rule:** "a" only goes with ONE thing. `a gift` = one. `gifts` = more than one.
You cannot put both together.

### 4. PHRASES THAT SOUND TRANSLATED

| You wrote | Natural English |
|---|---|
| today morning | **this morning** |
| if we win in it | **if we won** |
| to talk a short break | **to take a short break** |
| the last this after cafe | **the last thing we did after the cafe** |
| went back to home | **went home** |

"Today morning" is a direct translation of "الصبح النهاردة". English says "this morning".

### 5. MISSING WORDS

- "that **will** always remember" → "that **I** will always remember" (you dropped the subject)

## WHAT TO DO NEXT TIME — 4 checks before you press "Check again"

1. **Count your full stops.** If 170 words has fewer than 8 full stops, stop and fix it.
   This one check alone will move your writing score.

2. **Read it out loud.** Every time your voice pauses — full stop. Every time.
   Your ear already knows where they go. Trust it.

3. **Circle every verb. Is it past?** The story is finished, so every verb is past.

4. **Search for the letter "a".** If the next word ends in "s", you have a mistake.
